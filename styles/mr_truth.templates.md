# Mr Truth — template guide

Mr Truth reads this file at the start of every video and hands it to the planner after the repo's own brief.
Edit it freely: where it disagrees with the repo's brief, this file wins. The templates and their rules come from
OmharRegidor's video-generator (TREATMENT-STANDARD.md and its template library).

Watch any template: `python mrtruth.py preview P03,P14` (or `preview all`).

## The rules that matter most

1. **The first shot always carries P03**, the opening title.
2. **The gallery strip appears once**, right after the hook, previewing four pictures the video shows later.
3. **Every spoken four-figure year gets P12 or P14.** P14 (spinning wheels) is for a date that lands fast, P12 for a quiet one.
4. **Chapters:** the first chapter turn in the first half takes chapter-title (its own black grid). Later turns take chapter-plate (a heading over the photo), at most 5.
5. **Words on screen are the narration's own words.** Every content word must be spoken in that shot or the two either side. The only exceptions are chapter headings and the framed-card caption.
6. **A label that names a picture** (P04, P05's picture label, P17, corner-label, framed-card) only goes on a real photograph that clearly shows that thing. **Never on an AI picture.**
7. **No portrait-shaped pictures.** No picture on more than two shots.
8. **Loose, never empty:** a template that doesn't fit falls back to a simpler one, or the shot stays a clean photograph with its slow move. A clean photograph is a finished shot.

## How pictures are found

**Searching.** Each shot gets up to three search rounds, and stops the moment one photo passes the picture judge (5 out of 10, or 6 when its template names what the picture is):
1. Wikimedia Commons with the name an archive files the subject under, then a web image search (Brave).
2. Only when round 1 came back with very little: the same subject, searched more broadly.
3. When the shot names a person, place or object and no exact photo was found: a different anchor from the same narration (the person, then the product or object, then the place, then the period setting).

**What goes on screen**, the first that exists:
1. the exact photo
2. the new anchor's photo
3. a contextual real photo (the right kind of thing; a shot that names nothing goes straight here)
4. an unused photo from a neighbouring shot
5. an AI picture, while under the video's AI limit (20)
6. over the limit, a real photo from earlier in the video (never within 3 shots, at most twice)

There are no text cards. Only an exact photo may carry a label that names what it shows. Photos visibly from after the film's period, and cartoon-style drawings, score low.

## Transitions and look (the repo's numbers)

Mostly hard cuts. About 1 in 5 cuts is a film burn (at most 4 a minute, at least 6 s apart, never more than 2 in a row). 5% are white flashes, 8% push cuts, 3% slides, and there's a dip to black at every chapter turn. No crossfades. Dust drifts over a few dark plain photographs. Sound: a camera shutter on a flash, a soft thud on a dip, mixed quietly under the voice. No subtitles.
