Sound effects in this folder are synthesised locally from filtered noise and tones (no third-party audio, nothing to license).
- counter-tick.wav: one mechanical counter click (E38 percent gauge plays one per change of the shown number).
- sub-boom.wav: deep 55->38 Hz boom, 0.6 s decay (E02 year drop starts with it).
- soft-whoosh.wav: 0.5 s band-swept noise whoosh, peaks at 0.15-0.25 s (E03 highlight sweep).
- type-click.wav: one typewriter key click (E13 plays one per word as it lands).
- shutter-click.wav: two-part camera shutter click (E32/E33 entrances; quiet, heard by Gemini, faint in the reference).
